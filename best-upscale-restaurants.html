The Best Upscale Restaurants in Ottawa for 2026

Ottawa's high-end dining scene has never had more range. If you're looking for something fancy — the kind of fine dining that turns a Tuesday into an occasion — the capital now has a genuine handful of kitchens operating at a level that rivals cities several times its size.

These are upscale rooms in every sense: considered service, ambitious tasting menus, and plating that treats every course like an event of its own. Whether you're planning a proposal, a milestone birthday, or simply want a night of serious, high-end cooking, the five restaurants below represent the very top of what Ottawa's fine dining world has to offer right now.

Each one takes a different approach to what "special occasion" dining can mean — from tasting menus built entirely around fermentation, to a room that treats every plate as a small piece of art. What they share is an unwillingness to compromise, and a level of ambition that puts them firmly in a class of their own.

Antheia

Sixteen seats, one uncompromising tasting menu

Few restaurants in the country commit as fully to a single idea as Antheia. Chef Briana Kim's fermentation-driven tasting menu unfolds course by course around an open kitchen, drawing on koji, garums, and preserves that have often been developing for months. It's high-end dining stripped of anything unnecessary — nothing on the plate that hasn't earned its place.

For anyone seeking a genuinely special occasion meal that doubles as a conversation piece for weeks afterward, this is as fancy and considered as Ottawa's fine dining scene gets.

Atelier

No menu — just an evolving tasting experience

Atelier remains the standard-bearer for high-end dining in this city. There's no à la carte, no fixed courses — just Chef Marc Lepine's ever-changing tasting menu, delivered plate by plate with the kind of confidence only two Canadian Culinary Championship titles can buy.

It's the most talked-about fine dining reservation in Ottawa, and for good reason: every visit is different, every course engineered to surprise. This is what fancy, special-occasion dining looks like when a kitchen refuses to repeat itself.

Gitanes

French sophistication on Elgin Street

Gitanes brings a distinctly French sensibility to Ottawa's high-end dining scene — refined plating, a serious wine program, and a room polished enough for any special occasion without ever tipping into stuffy. The kitchen has a delicate touch, favouring precision and restraint over spectacle.

It's the kind of upscale spot that works equally well for a milestone anniversary or a client dinner that needs to impress — fancy in every sense, but never intimidating.

Supply & Demand

A raw bar and house-made pasta program built for a proper night out

Supply & Demand earns its place among Ottawa's high-end dining destinations through sheer consistency of craft — a raw bar of impeccably sourced seafood, paired with a rotating lineup of house-made pastas that change with the seasons. It's upscale cooking that never feels showy for its own sake.

The room strikes an easy balance between fine dining polish and genuine warmth, making it a dependable pick whenever the occasion calls for something a little fancier than usual.

Stofa

Confident, ingredient-led tasting menus

Stofa has quietly become one of Ottawa's most trusted names in high-end dining, built on a tasting menu that lets Canadian ingredients speak for themselves rather than chasing trends. The kitchen's technique is exacting, but the result never feels overworked — every course arrives with a clear sense of purpose.

For a special occasion that calls for serious fine dining without unnecessary theatre, Stofa consistently delivers one of the most complete tasting menu experiences in the city.
